Eurorack modules

The same blocks as the minimodules, built the usual Eurorack way: the jacks, pots and switches on the board, the board behind its front panel, power from the bus. Both are layout studies so far, with their parts placed and their panels drawn. They wait for the minimodules to prove the circuits first.

VCO 10HP

The full VCO as a 10 HP Eurorack module: PWM, sync, a three-mode sub-octave, triangle and sine.

In design: parts placed, not routed yet

VOICE

A semi-modular voice in 3U, 42 HP: the series' analog modules on one board, normalled to play with a gate and a pitch CV.

In design: parts placed, not routed yet

synthunits is made by Jeremy Seitz in Luzern, Switzerland. The design isn't open source (yet); this site shows how it works.
